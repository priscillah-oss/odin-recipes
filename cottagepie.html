Classic Cottage Pie

What makes this classic cottage pie recipe so spectacular? Homemade meat sauce and plenty of cheese. Best of all, it's easy to make cottage pie ahead of time so dinner can be ready in a flash.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef: This combination creates the iconic flavor that defines traditional cottage pie. But you could go with all beef or all sausage, or ground chicken or ground turkey. (Vegetable cottage pie is also delightful as a meatless variation.)
  • Onions and garlic: These aromatics add depth of flavor to the meat sauce, enhancing the overall taste of the dish.
  • Carrots and peas: These vegetables add color, texture, and a touch of sweetness to the savory meat filling, making the dish more balanced and nutritious.
  • Potatoes: The mashed potatoes on top create a creamy, comforting layer that complements the savory meat filling below. They also help to keep the filling moist during baking.

How to make Cottage Pie

  1. Prepare the meat filling: Cook the ground beef with onions, garlic, carrots, and peas until the meat is browned and the vegetables are tender. Season with salt, pepper, and any desired herbs or spices.
  2. Make the mashed potatoes: Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash them with butter, milk, and seasonings until smooth and creamy.
  3. Assemble the pie: Spread the meat filling evenly in a baking dish, then top with the mashed potatoes, spreading them out to cover the filling completely. You can use a fork to create a decorative pattern on the surface of the mashed potatoes.
  4. Bake: Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and bake the cottage pie for about 25-30 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the filling is bubbling. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld together.

Tips for the Best Cottage Pie

  • Use a mix of ground beef and ground lamb for a richer flavor.
  • For extra creaminess, add a bit of cream cheese or sour cream to the mashed potatoes.
  • Make the meat filling a day ahead to allow the flavors to develop even more.
  • Top the mashed potatoes with a sprinkle of grated cheese for a deliciously cheesy crust.
