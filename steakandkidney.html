Steak and Kidney Pie Recipe

Classic Steak and Kidney Pie

What makes this classic steak and kidney pie recipe so spectacular? Homemade meat sauce and a flaky pastry crust. Best of all, it's easy to make steak and kidney pie ahead of time so dinner can be ready in a flash.

Ingredients

  • Steak and kidney: This combination creates the iconic flavor that defines traditional steak and kidney pie. You can use beef steak and lamb kidney for the best results.
  • Onions and garlic: These aromatics add depth of flavor to the meat sauce, enhancing the overall taste of the dish.
  • Carrots and peas: These vegetables add color, texture, and a touch of sweetness to the savory meat filling, making the dish more balanced and nutritious.
  • Pastry: A flaky pastry crust is essential for a perfect steak and kidney pie. You can use store-bought pastry or make your own for a more authentic taste.

How to make Steak and Kidney Pie

  1. Prepare the meat filling: Cook the steak and kidney with onions, garlic, carrots, and peas until the meat is browned and the vegetables are tender. Season with salt, pepper, and any desired herbs or spices.
  2. Make the pastry: Roll out the pastry and line a pie dish with it. You can also use the pastry to create a top crust for the pie.
  3. Assemble the pie: Spread the meat filling evenly in the pie dish, then cover with the pastry. Seal the edges and make a few slits on top to allow steam to escape.
  4. Bake: Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and bake the steak and kidney pie for about 45-50 minutes, or until the pastry is golden brown and the filling is bubbling. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld together.

Tips for the Best Steak and Kidney Pie

  • Use a mix of beef steak and lamb kidney for a richer flavor.
  • For extra creaminess, add a bit of cream or butter to the meat filling.
  • Make the meat filling a day ahead to allow the flavors to develop even more.
  • Top the pie with a sprinkle of grated cheese for a deliciously cheesy crust.
